Memberships and fellowships

Grades held, and who conferred them

Membership evidence is only worth what the conferring body's own record says. Each entry below names the body, the grade, the date and, where the body publishes one, the verification route.

IEEE Senior Member

  • Elevated to Senior Member of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers in June 2026, member number 102303821. IEEE describes Senior Member as a grade awarded to those who have demonstrated significant performance over a sustained period, assessed by a panel of peers, and notes that it is held by a minority of the total membership. Source: Senior Member certificate (image) · IEEE, Senior Member grade ↗

The Scholars Academic and Scientific Society

  • Eminent Fellow Member (SEFM), conferred in June 2026 as a lifetime honour. The Society states that its fellowships follow a nomination and recommendation process reviewed by its Eminent Fellows. Source: Certificate (image) · Society membership verification page ↗

Royal Statistical Society

  • Fellow of the Royal Statistical Society, membership number 263939, since July 2026. The Society is the United Kingdom professional body for statistics, founded in 1834 and incorporated by Royal Charter. It styles its members Fellows, and welcomed me to Fellow membership in July 2026. Source: Membership email (image) · Confirmation letter (image) · Royal Statistical Society ↗

Soft Computing Research Society

  • Fellow of the Soft Computing Research Society, Fellow Certificate number SCRS/Fellow/2360. The Society is an international professional society for researchers and practitioners in soft computing and its applications. Source: Fellow certificate (image) · Soft Computing Research Society ↗

Each recognition carries the conferring body's own record, and, where the body issued one, a copy of the document it sent me.
