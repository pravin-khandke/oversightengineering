Keynote

  • The Trust Gap: Architecting Autonomous AI Systems for Real-World Accountability. Keynote at the 2026 IEEE 5th World Conference on Applied Intelligence and Computing, Jabalpur, India, 29 and 30 August 2026. The talk covers how autonomous agent systems fail once they leave a demonstration, and how to verify them before they are trusted. It also argues that the sustainability cost of running them belongs in the same conversation as their accuracy. Source: Conference programme and keynote list ↗

Conference sessions

  • The Naive AI Trap: Why Finance-Grade Extraction Needs Hybrid Agents. Extract Summit 2026, Brazos Hall, Austin, Texas, 7 and 8 October 2026, on the second day. The session covers why extraction that has to be correct, as opposed to merely plausible, needs hybrid agent designs rather than a single model in a loop. Source: Extract Summit programme ↗
  • Scalable Event-Driven Architectures for Distributed Retail Data Collection. Presented at IEEE ICCBI 2026, Dubai, June 2026, alongside the published paper. Source: IEEE ICCBI 2026 programme ↗
