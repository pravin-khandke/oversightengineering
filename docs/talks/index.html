Keynote

29 and 30 August, Jabalpur, India

The Trust Gap: Architecting Autonomous AI Systems for Real-World Accountability

IEEE 5th World Conference on Applied Intelligence and Computing

How autonomous agent systems fail once they leave a demonstration, and how to verify them before they are trusted. The talk also argues that the sustainability cost of running them belongs in the same conversation as their accuracy.

The Naive AI Trap: Why Finance-Grade Extraction Needs Hybrid Agents

Why extraction that has to be correct, as opposed to merely plausible, needs hybrid agent designs rather than a single model in a loop. The session is on the second day.
