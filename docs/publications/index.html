Peer-reviewed papers, with the record linked

Three papers have been through peer review. Each entry links to the published record, where the title, the authors and the DOI can be checked. Talks and technical articles have their own pages.

Peer-reviewed papers

Designing Adaptive Human-in-the-Loop Interfaces for Enhanced Collaborative Incident Management

International Journal of Computer Applications, volume 187, number 118, 2026

Examines interface architectures in which human operators and AI systems jointly manage complex incident workflows, with cognitive task allocation shifting according to the operator's state and the system's own confidence. It is the published statement of the human-in-the-loop pattern I apply to production systems.

Source: IJCA article record ↗

Scalable Event-Driven Architectures for Distributed Retail Data Collection using ActiveMQ Artemis

2026 5th International Conference on Computer Networks, Big Data and IoT (ICCBI), Dubai, 2026

DOI 10.1109/ICCBI68589.2026.11619835

Examines high-throughput messaging topologies for multi-site data aggregation, using ActiveMQ Artemis as the backbone for event propagation across independently governed retail nodes. Presented in Dubai in June.

Source: IEEE Xplore record ↗

An Event-Driven Context-Aware Framework for Margin-Preserving Dynamic Pricing in High-Velocity Retail Commerce Systems

2026 International Workshop on Intelligent Systems (IWIS), Ulsan, Korea, 2026

DOI 10.1109/IWIS70716.2026.11667738

Co-authored with Jayakumar Ramalingam and Jose Prabhu Michael Singarayan. Proposes a context-aware pricing framework that adjusts retail prices in real time from streaming demand and inventory signals while preserving margin targets. Presented in Ulsan in August.

Source: IEEE Xplore record ↗

Open source

Two of the papers have a companion repository. The children's guide is the third public repository.

  • Companion implementation of the IEEE ICCBI paper on event-driven retail data collection with ActiveMQ Artemis. It simulates retail events from 50 virtual store locations. Source: scalable-event-driven-retail repository ↗
